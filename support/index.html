Help with INR Wizard.

Find practical steps for common app questions or contact the developer about a technical problem.

Before restoring or resetting

Create an encrypted backup, confirm that you know its password, and keep the file in a location you trust. Restoring can replace the journal on this iPhone. Resetting removes its data.

If a reminder does not appear

Open iPhone Settings, select Notifications, then INR Wizard. Confirm that notifications and Time Sensitive Notifications are allowed. Also review the medication and schedule time inside INR Wizard.

If an entry looks incorrect

Compare it with the original meter, laboratory report, prescription label, or clinician instruction. Correct the record in the appropriate INR, dose, or schedule screen.

When reporting a problem

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, INR Wizard version, what you tapped, what you expected, and what happened. Do not email patient records, backup passwords, or unnecessary health details.

Contact the developer

Email kudige@icloud.com

Support messages are reviewed for app-related questions. This address is not monitored as an emergency medical service.

!

Do not send urgent medical questions by email. For bleeding, injury, a concerning INR result, or questions about an anticoagulant dose, contact the appropriate healthcare professional or local emergency service. Do not change medication based on INR Wizard.
