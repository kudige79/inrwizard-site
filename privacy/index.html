Privacy Policy

Your health journal stays under your control.

INR Wizard is designed to keep live records on your iPhone. There are no developer accounts, advertising trackers, or analytics services behind the app.

Effective 30 August 2026 INR Wizard 1.0 Beta
●

Stored on your iPhone

Your live journal is stored locally inside INR Wizard. The app does not operate a cloud account or developer server for your records.

◒

No tracking or advertising

INR Wizard does not contain advertising, analytics trackers, or third-party marketing SDKs, and it does not sell personal information.

↑

Sharing is your choice

Reports and encrypted backups leave the app only when you choose a destination through Apple’s standard sharing or Files interface.

Information handled by the app

INR Wizard can store information that you enter, including a patient name, date of birth, height, weight, clinician details, INR measurements, anticoagulant information, prescribed schedules, dose records, symptom check-ins, reminder settings, and notes. BMI is calculated on the device from the height and weight entered in the profile.

This information is sensitive health information. It is kept in the app’s protected local storage and is not transmitted to the developer.

Data collection and tracking

The developer does not collect data from INR Wizard. The app has no user account, advertising, cross-app tracking, analytics service, or developer-operated server. It does not use HealthKit or automatically synchronize the journal through CloudKit.

Diagnostic information may be available to Apple if you separately choose to share device analytics or TestFlight feedback with Apple. Apple controls that processing under its own privacy terms.

Reports and sharing

PDF reports are created on the device. A report leaves INR Wizard only when you choose to share, save, print, or send it. The recipient or service you select then handles that copy under its own privacy and security practices.

Reports can contain patient, clinician, INR, treatment, dose, and symptom information. Review the destination carefully before sharing.

Encrypted backup and restore

Backup files are encrypted using the password you provide. INR Wizard does not transmit, store remotely, or recover that password. If the password is lost, the developer cannot restore the backup for you.

You select where an exported backup is saved using Apple’s Files interface. If you choose iCloud Drive or another storage provider, that provider receives and stores the encrypted file under its own terms. The developer does not receive it.

Notifications

Medication reminders and recorded-range alerts are scheduled locally on the iPhone. They may include information you entered, such as a medicine name, scheduled time, or recorded result. You control whether notification previews appear on the Lock Screen in iOS Settings.

INR Wizard may request permission for Time Sensitive notifications. It does not use Critical Alerts. Notifications can be changed or disabled at any time.

Protection and retention

INR Wizard uses Apple’s complete file-protection class for its app data. Device security also depends on your iPhone passcode, Apple Account security, operating-system updates, and the destinations to which you export files.

Records remain until you edit or delete them, reset the app, or remove the app from the iPhone. Exported reports and manually saved backups are separate files and must be deleted from their destinations by you.

Your choices

  • Review, correct, or delete records from within INR Wizard.
  • Change reminder and notification permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Create an encrypted backup before replacing or erasing data.
  • Use Reset All Data when preparing the app for another person.
  • Delete exported reports and backups from the places you saved them.

Medical safety

INR Wizard is a patient-entered record and reporting tool. It does not diagnose a condition, determine whether a result is safe, calculate or recommend a medication dose, or replace a clinician, laboratory test, prescription label, or emergency service.

Do not change Warfarin or another anticoagulant based on the app. When a treatment decision depends on an INR result, follow the instructions of the appropriate healthcare professional and obtain laboratory confirmation when advised.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when INR Wizard’s data practices materially change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.
