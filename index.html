INR, in plain language

What is INR, and how does it relate to blood?

Blood clotting is the body’s natural way of limiting bleeding after an injury. A prothrombin time, or PT, test measures how many seconds it takes a clot to form in a blood sample. The International Normalised Ratio turns that PT result into a standardised number, making results easier to compare across test methods.

INR testing is commonly used to help a healthcare professional monitor Warfarin, an anticoagulant medicine. A sample may be taken from a vein in a laboratory or from a fingertip with a home meter. The result must be considered with the person’s condition, medicine, and clinician-provided target range.

A detailed patient journal

Everything needed to keep an organised record

Each feature is designed to record information faithfully without turning the app into a treatment or dosing tool.

INR

Home and laboratory results

Record each result with its date and time, identify it as a home-meter or laboratory reading, and add optional method, meter, strip, laboratory, or note details.

±

Descriptive comparisons

Review paired readings with the signed numerical and percentage difference. The app keeps measured results distinct and does not call either one a corrected or true INR.

↕

Clinician-provided range

Save the current lower and upper values provided by a clinician. Measured results outside that recorded range are highlighted in colour and text, with optional local alerts.

ID

Patient and care-team details

Keep date of birth, height, weight, multiple physicians, specialties, practices, and contact details together for personalised reports. BMI is calculated on the device.

Rx

Multiple anticoagulant records

Record medicine name, oral or injection route, prescriber, label strengths, dosage instructions, and treatment dates. INR Wizard never chooses a tablet combination.

7d

Warfarin schedule history

Transcribe a seven-day clinician-provided Warfarin schedule with its dose time. Revised or stopped schedules keep effective dates so earlier dose records remain understandable.

⏱

Time-specific reminders

Choose a reminder at the recorded dose time or at a separate notification time. Reminders are scheduled locally and can use Apple’s Time Sensitive notification setting.

✓

Actual Dose Ledger

Record today or an earlier date as taken as scheduled, taken in a different amount, skipped, missed, or uncertain. Planned amounts and actual records stay separate.

+

Symptoms and safety events

Use quick selections to record symptoms or injuries noticed while taking Warfarin. Urgent selections direct the user to professional or emergency help without making a diagnosis.

PDF

Date-range PDF reports

Choose 7, 30, or 90 days, or a custom range. Preview a report containing patient, clinician, INR, treatment, schedule, actual dose, and symptom information before sharing it.

ಅ

⌁

Backup, restore, and reset

Create a password-encrypted full-journal backup, restore a validated backup, or use the protected reset flow when preparing the app for another patient.

An important boundary

Records and reports, not medical recommendations

INR Wizard does not measure INR, verify an entered result or prescription, diagnose a condition, determine whether a value or symptom is safe, calculate a medication dose, choose tablet combinations, or provide catch-up, hold, resume, increase, or decrease instructions.

Check important entries against the original meter, laboratory report, label, or clinician instruction. Consult the appropriate healthcare professional before making a medical decision or changing medication. For severe symptoms, uncontrolled bleeding, or an emergency, contact local emergency services.

Help when you need it

Quick answers for common questions

The Help Centre explains everyday tasks in the same plain language used throughout the app.

Does INR Wizard measure or interpret my INR?

No. You copy a measured result into the app. INR Wizard organises the record and can compare it with a clinician-provided range, but it cannot decide whether the result is safe.

Can the app tell me how much Warfarin to take?

No. The app records a schedule that you transcribe from a clinician or prescription source. It never calculates, recommends, or changes a dose.

Can I add an older INR result or dose record?

Yes. Both INR results and actual-dose records can be entered retrospectively with the date and time that apply to the record.

What should I do if something is urgent?

Do not wait for the app or send an urgent medical question to support. Contact the appropriate healthcare professional or local emergency service.
